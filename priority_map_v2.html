Moving Preferences and Climate Change Mapping Exercise

First, you will allocate points across factors that matter to you — such as climate hazards, cost of living, place type, and more. You will have a total of 100 points to allocate. This will generate a map based on your priorities.

After seeing your map, you will have the opportunity to revise your point allocations or make manual adjustments directly on the map before submitting your final response.
